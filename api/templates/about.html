About Us

Bringing Luxury Spa
to Your Doorstep

GlowMobile Spa was founded with a simple belief: self-care shouldn't require a commute. We bring expertly trained therapists, premium products, and the full spa experience to your home.

Our Story

GlowMobile was born in 2020 when our founder, Amara Chen, realized how many people skipped self-care because of busy schedules, long commutes, and crowded spa waiting rooms.

She assembled a team of the city's finest therapists, invested in portable professional equipment, and created a luxury spa that comes to you — whether that's your home, office, hotel, or event venue.

Today, GlowMobile serves hundreds of clients monthly, from busy professionals to new mothers, bridal parties to corporate wellness events.
